## Core Rules

1. **Templates only** — every document starts from `99_Templates/`.
2. **Stable IDs** — every document has a unique `id`; never reuse or rename one.
3. **Link everything** — no orphans; every document links its parent hub.
4. **Core Obsidian only** — no community plugins. Bases, Templates, Canvas, Properties are enough.
5. **English framework, any-language content** — Codex, templates, frontmatter keys, IDs are English; document bodies can be in any language.
