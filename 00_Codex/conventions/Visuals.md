# Visuals

Graph view colors by type and risk. Shipped in `.obsidian/graph.json`.

| Query | Color | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| `path:00_Codex` | ⚪ gray | Constitution |
| `tag:#type/rule` | 🔵 blue | Rules |
| `tag:#type/skill` | 🟣 purple | Skills |
| `tag:#type/context` | 🟢 green | Context |
| `tag:#risk/critical` | 🔴 red | Critical risk — always wins |

Members may add per-domain colors (`tag:#domain/py`) in their own vault.
